# simPl privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-04. Applies to the Android simPl Reader application
(`io.github.tikkaaa3.simpl`) and its optional features.

simPl has no account system, advertising, analytics or telemetry. The application
does not upload your books, reading history, notes, searches or dictionary queries
to the project maintainers.

## Data on your device

Imported documents are copied into app-private storage. Reading positions,
bookmarks, highlights, notes, shelves and preferences are stored locally.
Disposable conversions and dictionary downloads use private cache/storage.
Removing a book removes its managed copy while retaining saved annotations;
it does not delete your original file. Uninstalling the app or clearing its
storage removes app-private data. Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled.

## Optional network and other applications

Dictionary packages are downloaded only when requested. The package host receives
ordinary connection information such as your IP address and requested package.
Definitions and lookup queries are processed locally. Package hosts are identified
in the dictionary catalog; hosting providers apply their own privacy policies.

Read aloud passes book text to your chosen Android speech engine. simPl prefers
offline voices. A network voice is used only after explicit consent and may send
text and connection information to the engine's provider. Its privacy practices
are independent of simPl. You can stop playback and choose another voice.

Android's file picker lets you choose local or cloud document providers. Providers
may process imports or exports according to their own policies. simPl accesses
only files or folders you select. Sharing selected text or exported notes gives
the content you choose to the receiving application.

## Backups and exports

Backups and exported notes are saved to destinations you choose. Portable ZIP
backups are not encrypted; full backups can contain complete books and notes.
Choose a trusted destination and protect or delete exported files yourself.
Exports are outside app-private storage and can remain after uninstallation.
Restoring a backup replaces the app's reading profile after your confirmation.

## Contact and changes

The source, issue tracker and maintainer contact are available at
[Tikkaaa3/simPl-reader](https://github.com/Tikkaaa3/simPl-reader).
Use the [issue tracker](https://github.com/Tikkaaa3/simPl-reader/issues) for
questions; do not attach private books or notes to public reports. Policy changes
will update this page and its effective date.
