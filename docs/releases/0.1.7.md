# simPl 0.1.7

A calmer reading screen: a title bar that stays, tools that get out of the way, a
progress line with chapters, and a theme for each book.

## New since 0.1.6

- **A title bar that stays.** While you read, the title bar keeps the Library
  button, the book's title, search, Settings and the window buttons on screen.
  The reading tools are a small pill of icons under it: contents, the page field,
  find, listen, notes, **Aa** and light or dark. The pill hides as soon as you
  scroll or turn a page, and comes back when you point at the title bar. The pin
  in the title bar, or **F8**, keeps it on screen, and simPl remembers that.
  Fullscreen (**F11**, or its button in the title bar) hides everything; move the
  pointer to the top of the screen to bring both back.
- **A progress line with chapters.** A thin line along the bottom shows how far
  you are, cut into the book's chapters. Point at it to see the page you are on
  and the chapter and page a click would jump to. **Settings → Appearance →
  Progress line** hides it.
- **A theme for each book.** **Aa** in the reading tools sets the theme, font,
  size, spacing and margins of the open book. **Settings → Appearance** sets the
  theme every other book uses.
- **Read aloud from here.** Right-click text, a highlight or a place on the page
  and choose **Read aloud from here**.
- **Keep turning the wheel.** Push the wheel a little past the end of a Book page
  to go on to the next page, or past its top to go back.
- **Page turns from the edges.** Click anywhere along the left or right edge of
  the reading area to turn the page.

## Improved

- A PDF in Book view follows the reading theme, font, text size, spacing and
  margins, and keeps the PDF's own page boundaries.
- Find and the read-aloud player float over the page. Contents opens in a drawer
  on the left. A drawer sits beside the page while the page still fits there,
  and opens over it otherwise.
- Settings has six sections: Reading, Appearance, Listen, Translation, Library &
  data and Shortcuts. **F1** opens the shortcut list.
- The Library has one header row, which gives the books more room.
- The paper is the lightest surface in every theme, and a selected control looks
  different from a focused one.
- The scrollbar hides while you read and shows when you scroll or point at the
  right edge.

## Fixed

- Changing the theme or text settings in the middle of a long paragraph no
  longer turns back a page.
- The **Open file**, **Add Document** and folder dialogs no longer hang on the
  Downloads folder, and the window stays responsive while one is open.
- A second press on the find tool closes find.
- A click on the progress line jumps in a maximized or fullscreen window.
- Closing a book with the page field focused no longer keeps the next book's
  tools on screen.
- Switching light or dark in the first moment after starting simPl no longer
  resets the other settings.

## Changed shortcuts

- **F8** keeps the reading tools on screen. It no longer hides the title bar.
- **F1** opens **Settings → Shortcuts**.

## Upgrading from 0.1.6

Install over the existing version. Your library, preferences, reading positions,
notes and downloaded dictionaries are kept.

- Every book starts with the theme you had chosen. A theme chosen from **Aa**
  now applies to that book only.
- A backup includes the new settings and each book's own theme. A backup made
  by 0.1.6 restores as before.
- If you go back to 0.1.6 and change a setting there, the pinned tools, the
  hidden progress line and the books' own themes are dropped.

## Known issues

- An EPUB with an empty chapter file does not open
  ([#37](https://github.com/Tikkaaa3/simPl-reader/issues/37)).
- In a normal window, a click exactly on the progress line starts a window
  resize. Click just above it
  ([#39](https://github.com/Tikkaaa3/simPl-reader/issues/39)).

## Downloads

- **`simPl-0.1.7-windows-x64-setup.exe`**: per-user Windows installer with
  PDFium and notices included.
- **`simPl-0.1.7-windows-x64-portable.zip`**: extract the whole folder and run
  `simPl.exe`. Keep `pdfium.dll` and `third-party` beside it. Portable and
  installed copies share the same Windows-profile library, so only one of them
  runs at a time.
- **`SHA256SUMS`** and **`release.json`**: checksums and installer metadata.

Windows x64 is the current target. The installer is unsigned. Dictionary packs
remain optional downloads. Reading and installed dictionary lookups work
offline. PDF Book view needs a usable text layer and does not perform OCR.
